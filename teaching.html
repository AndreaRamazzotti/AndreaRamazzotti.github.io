Teaching

Current and past courses.

Universitas Mercatorum (2025–)

I am the convenor of two courses, Economic History for undergraduate students and Global History of Commercial Relations for master’s students. Among these teaching duties, I have produced over two hundred pages of original lecture notes across both courses. I regularly advise master’s and undergraduate theses.

University of Naples Federico II (2023–2025)

I taught two modules on the Applied Micro Reading Group of the Naples PhD in Economics: Quantitative Economic History and Historical Economics; Labor Market Institutions and Minimum Wage. I also taught sessions of microeconomics and applications to first-year undergraduates in Economics and Business (in Italian), and was a guest lecturer in labour economics for undergraduate students on the topic of the minimum wage (November 2023).

London School of Economics (2019–2023)

Over several years I taught a range of courses at the LSE, including EH101 (The Internationalisation of Economic Growth, 1870 to the Present), EH390 (Stata Workshop for Dissertations in Economic History), and modules of EC204 (Financial Markets and the Global Economy at the LSE Summer School) and EH240 (Business and Economic Performance since 1945: Britain in International Context). I was a mentor for new PhD students approaching teaching in the Economic History Department in 2020–21, and in 2021 was nominated by some of my EH101 students for the LSE teaching awards (category “excellent feedback and communication”).
